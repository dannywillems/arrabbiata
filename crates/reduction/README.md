# reduction

Reductions of knowledge (Kothapalli and Parno, CRYPTO 2023): relations as
objects, reductions between them as morphisms, with composition, products
and the trivial relation `R_top`. An argument of knowledge is a reduction to
`R_top`, a folding scheme a reduction `R x R -> R`. `knowledge` is the
category itself; accumulation schemes and deciders are its morphisms.
